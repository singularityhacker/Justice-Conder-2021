How I Work

How I Work

Find the right problem

Understand the workflow, stakeholders, constraints, and cost of the current approach.

Connected to Highmark and UPMC — both started by questioning an existing delivery or migration plan.

Build a useful first version

Scope a practical intervention, connect the necessary systems, and get something concrete into users’ hands.

Connected to ClawBank and the Thrive agent-workflow work: ship a working path, then widen it.

Make it dependable and adopted

Address failure modes, document the workflow, teach the team, and measure what changes.

Connected to MyUPMC University and the handbook, and to the delivery-workflow loop from report to shipped fix.
