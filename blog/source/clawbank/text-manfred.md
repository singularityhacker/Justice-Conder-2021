## The other side of the coin

Everyone's watching the same side of the AI story: smarter models, bigger memory, more tools. And all of it locked behind a wall of setup that assumes you're technical.

The less explored side is removing friction. The current answer to "how do I get an agent working for me" is some version of: buy a dedicated machine, stand up a server, configure the runtime, keep a heartbeat alive. That's a hobby for people who already run servers. It filters the most powerful technology of our lifetime down to the people who least need help using it.

We think the frontier is the opposite direction. Take the most capable agent you can build and expose it through the lowest-friction surface that exists: a contact in your phone.
