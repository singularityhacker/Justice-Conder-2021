Quadratic Acceleration: First Light
===================================

Giveth is building the next evolution of web3 grants to support builders on Polygon.

Many great projects depend on the Web3 grant ecosystem for funding. Still, there is a principal-agent problem: Desperate for survival, projects cut corners, focus on hitting metrics over creating value, and chase grants from competing ecosystems.

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/cc50f164acfdc34826ff5a538179518e34cdad79730f7e4a50413d4e74eec9e0.jpg)

What is the alternative to grant hopping? Tokenization! Tokenization realigns stakeholders and is a foundation for long-term self-reliance with the right supply & demand dynamics. But many small projects struggle to tokenize because:

*   They lack the capital to create liquidity 💰
    
*   Trust is hard to come by in crypto 🧐
    

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/2cfb0a670be6ee54c4de9b12d273230869679c13485d4404ab5db31cca510cf5.jpg)

Quadratic Acceleration (q/acc) solves this. But how?!

1.  Our team will select projects & give them $MATIC to launch their own Augmented Bonding Curves.
    
2.  Communities will have the opportunity to support their teams in QF-esque rounds.
    
3.  Donations will mint project tokens for supporters, and the rest will flow to the team.
    

Quadratic Acceleration aligns incentives for ecosystems, communities, and teams in web3; it’s also the foundation for Gurves. IYKYK 😉

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/9f1c0ee76ea053a9ab935c30fe147151900493968674922a88ecb08d442ef9d6.gif)

The q/acc protocol resolves the irresolvable because it:

*   Frees projects from grant-hopping 🥵
    
*   Realigns teams & ecosystems 🤝
    
*   Launches new economies safely 💸
    
*   Offers built-in liquid tokenization 🌊
    

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/papyrus_images/d942c2b502567c917cc15a1d40512d56d1a05f18cda8ac50027456ca2bd49c57.jpg)
