ClawBank Introduces Agent-First Limitless Prediction Markets

Originally published at https://clawbank.co/blog/limitless-prediction-markets/

The #1 prediction market on Base is now available through ClawBank and one text away: ~700 live markets, zero gas, zero deposits, and your funds never leave your wallet.

Text Manfred "what are the odds the Fed cuts rates this month?" and he searches roughly 700 live markets, pulls the actual order book, and quotes you the real price: best bid, best ask, last trade. If you like the odds, tell him how much. Your wallet signs the order, and you're in.

You never opened an app. You never bridged, never bought ETH, never deposited a cent anywhere. The whole trade happened in a text thread, and the money is still sitting in your own wallet until your order matches.

The Limitless Market

Markets are hosted by our partner Limitless Exchange, the #1 prediction market on Base: over $3.5B in volume, 70k+ monthly active traders, and an order book that lives fully on-chain. At any given time, that's 600 to 700 active markets across 44 categories. Politics, the economy, earnings, company news, commodities, crypto, tennis, football, NBA, F1, esports. If people are arguing about it somewhere, there's probably a market on it.

As of today, the biggest prediction venue on Base is reachable from a text message, and by any agent with an API key. Same books, same prices, same on-chain settlement as every other trader there; your orders rest on the public book like anyone else's.

Three ways in, all backed by the same code:

  1. Chat. Ask Manfred anything, get live odds, bet conversationally.
  2. Web App. Browse by volume, filter by category, and search semantically.
  3. CLI / MCP / API. Third-party agents get exactly what Manfred gets.

Low No Friction

A person with no crypto background can text Manfred, create an account, add some money, and be trading prediction markets in the same thread. No seed phrases, no gas, no bridge, no exchange signup.

The plumbing that makes this work stays invisible on purpose. Your first trade quietly registers your wallet with the venue (your wallet signs one message; no browser, no captcha, no email). The one-time USDC approval is gas-sponsored. After that, every order is just a signature from your own wallet. You need zero ETH, forever.

And because Manfred can already shop the x402 resource market, your research lives in the same thread as your bet. Ask him what's moving in some corner of the world; he pays a few cents for a live data feed, reads it, and quotes you the market it's relevant to. Paid intel in, and priced opinion out.

Bring Your Own Agent

Builders can bring their own agent as well. Everything above is agent-native from day one. The API has full parity with chat and is all documented at https://app.clawbank.co/docs.

So the workflow can be: bootstrap an account for your agent, fund it, cap its budget with the spending limits we shipped in the policy engine, and let it see how it performs. It can research markets with paid x402 feeds, quote the book, and place bounded bets on its own judgment, all inside the allowance you sized.

An agent with a wallet, a research budget, and a market for its opinions. This is the new economic organism ClawBank is enabling.

ClawBank Benefits

ClawBank is a Limitless referral partner, which means a share of the taker fees our users generate flows back to us, paid out daily in USDC. You pay exactly what you'd pay trading on Limitless directly; the venue just shares a slice with the platform that brought you.

And 100% of that referral revenue goes to buying back CLAWBANK. Every bet placed through ClawBank, by a human in a text thread or an agent on the API, becomes buy pressure on the token.

The Agent Exosuit is Growing

Legal formation gave agents a body. Records gave them a memory. Contracts let them make promises. x402 resources gave them a market. Headless Trading gave them a trading desk. Predictions give them skin in the game: a way to have an opinion about the future and be paid, or corrected, for it.
