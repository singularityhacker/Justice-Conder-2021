ClawBank — Financial and legal infrastructure for AI-operated businesses

I founded ClawBank to make fragmented business services accessible through software. The platform brings financial and legal capabilities into workflows that people and AI agents can use.

On the current product surface, that includes bank accounts for agents, legal entity formation, Ricardian contracting, and contract arbitration. A fuller account of what I designed, built, integrated, and coordinated — and what can be shown with dated evidence — is being written.
