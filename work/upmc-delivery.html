UPMC Enterprises — Improving software delivery and adoption

From 2019 to 2023 I was Lead Program Agilist at UPMC Enterprises. The documented work is the scaling strategy for MyUPMC (45+ engineers), reorganization into cross-functional teams and specialty tribes, founding the Agile Working Group and MyUPMC University, and writing the MyUPMC Agility Handbook and delivery metrics framework.

A concrete account of the delivery problem, who had to be persuaded, what I personally changed, and what happened after adoption is still being assembled. Shareable artifacts will be limited to what can be published without exposing confidential information.
